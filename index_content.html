We are an interdisciplinary team at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research Hyderabad, India. Our group uses computer-based approaches to study the quantum-mechanical principles that govern molecules and materials. We focus on how electronic structure and molecular geometry control bonding, reactivity, spectroscopy, biomolecular interactions, excited states, and materials properties. Our work depends on high-performance computation. Maintained by
